# Engineering status and acceptance gaps

## Delivered connected prototype

Four distinct condensed teaching routes; versioned profiles; canonical wafer
identity and layer state; persistent server sessions; route dispatch; carrier
checks; machine controls; utility faults; holds and disposition; procedure audit;
first-answer knowledge checks; report export; direct bench navigation and an
accessible floor-plan fallback. This is a connected training prototype.

## Still required by the industrial blueprint

1. **Reference engineering:** approved per-node process flows, materials, design
   rules, wafer handling and equipment specifications. Current routes omit most
   masking, implants, isolation, contacts, interconnect levels and device physics.
2. **Physics:** replace target/thickness bookkeeping and checkpoint-only steps
   with validated models, calibrated uncertainties and provenance. Couple all
   thermal budgets, materials, defects and metrology consistently.
3. **Equipment fidelity:** equipment-specific operational and emergency sequences,
   door/vacuum access interlocks, maintenance, chamber aging, calibration, gas and
   chemical delivery, exhaust/abatement and independent sensor faults.
4. **Facility/3D:** engineering-reviewed bay layout and dimensions, realistic
   equipment assets, measured human ergonomics, cleanroom access geometry,
   contamination transport, real carrier loading/robots and AMHS scheduling.
   The procedural geometry is a functional visual prototype, not an OEM model.
5. **MES:** multi-wafer lots, concurrent shared equipment, job queues, authorized
   rework and review disposition, durable user identity, instructor permissions,
   session listing/recovery and retention policies.
6. **Training:** SME-approved SOP-linked scoring, secure instructor review and
   supervised normal/abnormal scenario validation. Current reports are activity
   evidence, not validated competency assessments.
7. **Release evidence:** live PostgreSQL/Vercel integration tests, WebGL visual
   acceptance on target desktops/mobile devices, accessibility/performance
   testing, industrial benchmark comparisons and standards review.
8. **Digital twin integration:** separately authorized, isolated synchronization
   with trusted real-equipment data. No real-equipment interfaces are enabled.

Do not label this prototype industry-ready, production-qualified or a
production-connected digital twin. These gaps are release requirements, not
cosmetic follow-ups.
